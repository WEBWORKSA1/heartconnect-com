About HeartConnect.com

Our mission is simple: help more people understand their hearts, act on warning signs early, and connect to the right care faster.

Why we exist

Cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death worldwide, yet much of it can be prevented or treated well when caught early. Good information is out there — but it is scattered across hospital sites, charities, government portals and videos, often written in technical language. People facing a diagnosis also struggle to find the right specialist, understand their options, or compare costs.

HeartConnect brings these pieces together: plain-language guides, free self-check tools, curated videos, daily news, a supportive community and a free concierge that helps people find appropriate care.

What we do

  • Educate — clear, regularly updated guides based on recognised clinical and public-health sources.
  • Empower — private, in-browser tools to understand and track your numbers.
  • Connect — the Heart Care Concierge helps patients and families find specialists, second opinions and treatment options.
  • Engage — community circles, stories and monthly contests that make heart-healthy habits stick.

Our standards

We follow a written editorial policy: we cite reputable sources, avoid sensational claims, separate advertising from editorial content, and update articles when guidance changes. HeartConnect does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment — see our medical disclaimer.

How we're funded

HeartConnect is free to use. We are supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, partner referrals through the care concierge, and voluntary donations. None of these influence the content of our guides.

Independence

HeartConnect.com is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any hospital, charity, medical society, government agency or other organisation that uses a similar name, or any organisation referenced on this site. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.
