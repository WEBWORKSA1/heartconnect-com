Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HeartConnect.com ("HeartConnect", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit this website or use its features. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you submit: when you use a form (care concierge, newsletter, contact, contest, community, careers, donation pledge, advertising enquiry) we receive the details you enter, such as name, email, phone, country and your message. Please do not submit sensitive medical records through forms unless necessary for your request.
  • Tool data: calculator inputs and blood-pressure log entries are processed and stored only in your browser (local storage). We do not receive them.
  • Automatically collected data: like most websites, our host and third-party services may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed and referring URLs.

2. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences (such as dark mode and consent choices) and to show ads. See our Cookie Policy.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, we use a consent banner and, where required, a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are shown.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and provide the care concierge service.
  • To send newsletters you requested (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run contests, community circles and recruitment.
  • To process donation pledges and partnership enquiries.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the website, and to show ads.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: service providers who help us operate the site (for example, form-delivery, hosting, payment and email services); healthcare providers or coordinators you ask us to connect you with; and authorities where required by law.

5. Third-party services

We use services such as GitHub Pages (hosting), Google AdSense (advertising), YouTube (embedded videos in privacy-enhanced mode), FormSubmit (form delivery), PayPal (payments) and Google Fonts. Each has its own privacy policy.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

8. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions? Please use our contact form.
