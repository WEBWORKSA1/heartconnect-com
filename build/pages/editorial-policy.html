Editorial Policy

Sources

Our guides are based on publicly available guidance from recognised authorities such as the World Health Organization, national health institutes, government public-health agencies and major cardiology societies and heart charities. Each article lists key sources for further reading.

Writing and review

Content is written in plain language by the HeartConnect Editorial Team and checked against cited sources. We are building a panel of licensed clinicians to review content; reviewed articles will show the reviewer's name and credentials.

Updates and corrections

Each article shows an "updated" date. We review content regularly and update it when major guidance changes. If you spot an error, please contact us — we correct verified errors promptly.

Independence and advertising

Advertisers and sponsors have no influence over editorial content. Sponsored content is always clearly labelled. We do not accept ads for tobacco or unproven treatments.

Automated content

News headlines and video listings are gathered automatically from reputable public feeds and link to the original publisher. They are not edited by us and do not imply endorsement.
