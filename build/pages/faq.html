Frequently asked questions

What is HeartConnect.com?

HeartConnect.com is an independent heart-health website offering plain-language guides, free self-check tools, curated videos, daily news, community circles, contests and a free Heart Care Concierge that helps people find appropriate care.

Is HeartConnect a medical provider?

No. We provide general education and help with connections to care. We do not diagnose, treat or give personal medical advice. Always speak with a qualified clinician about your health.

Are the tools accurate?

Our calculators use widely published formulas and categories, such as adult blood-pressure categories and WHO BMI cut-offs. They are educational estimates and cannot replace a clinical assessment.

Is my tool data stored?

No. Calculator inputs and blood-pressure log entries stay in your own browser. Only information you choose to submit in a form is sent to us.

How does the Heart Care Concierge work?

You answer four short questions. A coordinator reviews your request and sends personalised options such as specialists, hospitals, second-opinion routes and cost information, usually within 24–48 hours. It is free for patients and families.

How are winners chosen in contests?

A judging panel scores entries on originality, heart-health value and clarity. Winners are contacted by email. No purchase or donation is required to enter.

Are donations tax-deductible?

HeartConnect.com is an independent project, not a registered charity, unless stated otherwise for your region, so donations may not be tax-deductible. They directly support operations, outreach, hiring and contest prizes.

Why do I see ads?

Advertising, including Google AdSense, helps keep everything free. Ads are clearly separated from content and never influence our editorial decisions. You can manage cookie choices at any time.

How often is content updated?

News and videos refresh automatically every day. Guides are reviewed regularly and updated when major guidance changes.
