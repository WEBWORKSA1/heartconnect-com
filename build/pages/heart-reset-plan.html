Your 30-Day Heart Reset Plan

One small, doable action a day. Tick them off, stack the wins, and bring your progress to your next check-up.

If you have a heart condition, diabetes, or haven't exercised for a while, check with your clinician before increasing activity.

Week 1 — Know your starting point

Day 1Measure your blood pressure (or get it checked) and write it down. Use our BP tracker.
Day 2Measure your waist at belly-button level. Check your waist-to-height ratio.
Day 3Take a 10-minute walk after your largest meal.
Day 4Read the sodium on three foods you eat often. Swap one high-salt item.
Day 5Add one extra serving of vegetables to lunch or dinner.
Day 6Set a consistent bedtime and turn screens off 30 minutes before.
Day 7Learn the heart-attack and stroke warning signs. Share them with family.

Week 2 — Eat for your heart

Day 8Choose a whole grain (oats, millet, brown rice, whole wheat) instead of a refined one.
Day 9Replace a sugary drink with water, unsweetened tea or coffee.
Day 10Cook one meal with no added salt — use lemon, garlic, herbs and spices.
Day 11Eat a handful of unsalted nuts or seeds as a snack.
Day 12Include beans, lentils or chickpeas in a meal.
Day 13Have oily fish, or a plant source of omega-3 such as flaxseed or walnuts.
Day 14Plan next week's meals with half of each plate as vegetables and fruit.

Week 3 — Move more

Day 15Walk briskly for 20 minutes (you can talk, but not sing).
Day 16Stand up and move for 2–3 minutes every 30 minutes of sitting.
Day 17Try 10 minutes of body-weight strength moves: sit-to-stands, wall push-ups.
Day 18Take the stairs or park farther away.
Day 19Walk 30 minutes — split into three 10-minute walks if easier.
Day 20Find your target heart-rate zones.
Day 21Do something active you enjoy: dance, cycle, swim, play.

Week 4 — Calm, sleep and next steps

Day 22Practise 5 minutes of slow breathing (around 6 breaths per minute).
Day 23If you smoke or vape, set a quit date and contact a quit service.
Day 24Keep alcohol low or skip it today.
Day 25Call a friend or family member — social connection supports heart health.
Day 26Learn Hands-Only CPR. Read our CPR guide.
Day 27Book overdue tests: cholesterol, blood sugar, kidney function.
Day 28Re-check your blood pressure and compare with Day 1.
Day 29Write down three habits you'll keep. Take the Heart Habits Score again.
Day 30Celebrate! Share your progress in our community or enter this month's contest.
