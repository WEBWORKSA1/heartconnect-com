Official rules (summary)

  1. Free to enter. No purchase or donation necessary. Void where prohibited.
  2. Open to individuals aged 18+ (or with parent/guardian consent where allowed by local law).
  3. One entry per person per contest. Entries must be your own original work and must not infringe anyone's rights.
  4. Entries close at 23:59 (UTC) on the last day of the month. Winners are chosen by a judging panel on originality, heart-health value and clarity.
  5. Winners are notified by email within 14 days and must reply within 14 days to claim a prize, or an alternate winner may be chosen.
  6. By entering you grant HeartConnect a non-exclusive licence to display your entry with credit. You keep your copyright.
  7. Prizes are non-transferable; equivalent substitutes may be offered. Winners are responsible for any local taxes.
  8. Contest content is not medical advice. Full terms are in our Terms of Use.
