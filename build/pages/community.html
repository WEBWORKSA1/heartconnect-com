Hearts connect here 🤝

Heart conditions can feel isolating. Our community brings together survivors, caregivers, families of children with heart conditions, and anyone who wants to protect their heart.

🫶

Survivor circle

For people living after a heart attack, bypass, stent, valve surgery or cardiac arrest. Monthly online meet-ups.

👪

Caregiver circle

Practical tips and emotional support for spouses, parents and children caring for a loved one.

👶

Little hearts

For families of children and adults with congenital heart disease — from diagnosis to adulthood.

⚡

Rhythm club

AFib, pacemakers, ICDs and ablations — learn from people who have been there.

💃

Women's heart circle

Heart disease is a leading killer of women. Talk symptoms, pregnancy, menopause and advocacy.

🏃

Move-More club

Monthly step challenges with leaderboards and prizes — every step counts.

Join a support circle

Tell us which circle you'd like to join. We'll send you the next meet-up date and community guidelines. Membership is free.

  • Moderated, respectful, judgement-free spaces
  • Expert guest sessions with clinicians and dietitians
  • No medical advice is given — just shared experience and support

Share your heart story

Your experience could help someone recognise a warning sign, ask a better question, or feel less alone. Selected stories are edited with your approval and published (first name or anonymously). Every published story enters our monthly story contest.

Please don't include other people's private details. We never publish without your final approval.
