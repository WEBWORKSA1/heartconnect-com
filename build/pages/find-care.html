The right heart care, without the guesswork

Questions to bring to any heart specialist

  1. What exactly is my diagnosis, and how sure are we?
  2. What are all my treatment options — including medicines and lifestyle changes only?
  3. What are the benefits and risks of each option for someone like me?
  4. What happens if I wait or choose not to have the procedure?
  5. How many of these procedures does this team perform each year?
  6. What will recovery look like, and is cardiac rehab included?
  7. What is the full cost estimate, including stents, devices, ICU days and follow-up?

Frequently asked

Is this service really free?

Yes. Requesting options through HeartConnect is free for patients and families. Some partner hospitals or providers may support the service; this never changes the cost you are quoted.

Are you a hospital or a doctor?

No. HeartConnect is an independent information and connection service. We don't diagnose or treat. All medical decisions are made between you and licensed clinicians.

What happens to my information?

Your details are used only to respond to your request and are shared only with the coordinator and, with your permission, the providers you choose. See our Privacy Policy.

Can you help with an emergency?

No. If you have chest pain, sudden breathlessness, fainting or stroke signs, call your local emergency number immediately.

Are you a healthcare provider or hospital partner?

We welcome partnerships with hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and rehab programs. Visit our Advertise & Partner page.
